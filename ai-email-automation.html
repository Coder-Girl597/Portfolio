THE PROJECT

Catch up without
reading every email.

Busy inboxes can make it hard to spot what matters. This automation is designed to gather incoming emails, create a short summary, and deliver the overview each morning at 9:00 AM.

Instead of starting the day by sorting through every message, you get a focused snapshot of the inbox and can decide what needs your attention first.

HOW IT WORKS

Three steps to a
clearer inbox.

A scheduled workflow turns a collection of messages into a short, useful daily overview.

01

Gather

At the scheduled time, the workflow checks the inbox for incoming emails to include in the digest.

02

Summarize

AI condenses the messages into key points, making the main information quicker to review.

03

Start the day informed

A concise summary is prepared for 9:00 AM, giving you a simple overview to guide your next steps.

THE OUTCOME

More clarity.
Less inbox time.
